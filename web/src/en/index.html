Miff

Know that your loved ones are okay, without bothering them.

Miff is an app for your circle of trust: family, friends, partner… You create or join a “Miff” with a secret code, you choose trusted places, and, if you agreed to it, your circle sees an automatic status when you arrive at or leave a place, with no message to write.

Sharing your location is always your choice: it only starts with your consent, Miff by Miff, and you can stop it at any time.
