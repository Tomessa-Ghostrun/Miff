Request the deletion of your account

You can delete your Miff account and the data linked to it whenever you want. Here are the two ways to do it.

In the app (the easiest way)

  1. Open the Miff app and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose to delete your account, then confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first, to check that it is really you.

Your account and the data linked to it are then deleted, as described in the Privacy policy.

If you created a Miff, deleting your account also deletes the Miffs you created, with their places, statuses and photos. The other members then no longer have access to them.

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in

Write to us at {{CONTACT}}, from the e-mail address of your Miff account.

  1. Use “Deletion of my Miff account” as the subject.
  2. Tell us how you sign in to Miff (e-mail and password, Google or Apple).
  3. We may ask you to confirm your identity, so that nobody can pretend to be you.

Write to request the deletion

We reply within one month at most.

Do you just want to stop sharing?

You do not have to delete your account to stop sharing your location: in the app Settings, you can stop sharing Miff by Miff, at any time. You can also write to us at the same address to get a copy of your data.

Learn more

The details of what is deleted and how long data is kept are in the Privacy policy.
