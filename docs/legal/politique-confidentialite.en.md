# Miff Privacy Policy

**Version 0.1 — last updated: November 4, 2026**

Miff is an app that lets you know your loved ones are okay, without bothering them. To make that work, Miff processes some personal data, including information related to your location. This page explains, simply, what is collected, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you stay in control.

## 1. The essentials in 10 points

1. **Miff keeps your last known location and, if you publish a photo, the place where it was taken: nothing else.** This means the latitude, the longitude and the time of the measurement, saved on our servers (Firebase, Paris region). There is no history of your trips: each new location replaces the previous one, and the place of a photo disappears with it (24 hours).
2. **This is not live tracking.** Your phone itself watches the trusted places. Your location is saved at certain moments (for example when you open or leave the app, after a significant move, or when you arrive at a trusted place), and your loved ones see the last saved location, with the time.
3. **Sharing is your choice, Miff by Miff.** Nothing is saved without your explicit consent, and you can stop at any time.
4. **Only the members of the same Miff who also share can see your location and the place of your photo**: to see other people's, you must share yours. The creator of the Miff has no more rights than the other members.
5. **Your location and your photo are erased** when you stop sharing, leave the Miff, are removed from the Miff, or when the Miff or your account is deleted. **After 24 hours**, the app only shows your location hidden, with its date, and your photo can no longer be read; they nevertheless stay stored until they are replaced or erased (see article 7).
6. **Miff needs "Always" location** (in the background, even when the app is closed) to work: this is what lets it notice your arrivals and departures without you having to open the app. Notifications, on the other hand, are optional: if you accept them, Miff records your phone's notification identifier so it can let you know when a loved one gives news (see article 3).
7. **Your photo is taken with the app's camera (never from your gallery), published with the place where it was taken, and visible to the members of your Miffs for 24 hours**, then it can no longer be read. You decide whether to publish it or not. Your loved ones who kept notifications turned on are told by a notification that shows only your first name (never the photo, nor its place). A member of your Miff can keep a copy of it (a screenshot, or saving the photo on their phone, only if they choose to): we cannot erase that copy (see article 4).
8. **Usage statistics and crash reports are turned on when you click "Join"** (or "Continue with Google") at sign-up (there is no checkbox), and you can turn them off at any time in Settings, as a single choice. Before that click, nothing is sent. **Your location is never sent to Google Analytics or Crashlytics.**
9. **No advertising, no selling of data.**
10. **One account, one phone at a time**, and you can delete your account from the app and write to us to exercise your rights.

## 4. Location and your position, explained

This is the most sensitive part, so here is everything.

**Why Miff asks for "Always" location (in the background, even when the app is closed).** To automatically tell your circle that you have arrived at a trusted place or left it, without you having to open the app. **Miff needs this permission ("Always", with precise location) to work**: until it is granted, the app asks for it again and the permission screen stays displayed. You can withdraw it at any time in your phone settings; the app will then ask for it again. Withdrawing this permission has no consequence on your account, but Miff cannot work without it. Notifications are optional.

**How it works.**
- Miff uses the "region monitoring" features of iOS and Android: your phone itself detects when you enter or leave a trusted place. To choose which places to watch (the 20 closest to you, because of a limit on phones), it reads its position at certain moments and keeps a copy on the phone.
- Miff **does not track your position continuously** and **does not build a history of your trips**.
- When your sharing is on, your phone writes your status (for example "at home") and your **last known location** in each Miff you share with. The app writes there at certain moments: when opened, when closed, after a significant move, when arriving at a trusted place. Your loved ones therefore see the last **saved** location, with the time it was measured: it may be several hours old and is not your live position.
- When you publish a photo, your phone measures your location at that moment and attaches it to the photo: this is "the place of your photo", described below.

**Your last known location, in detail.**
- **What is saved**: the latitude, the longitude and the time of the measurement, nothing else. No history: each location replaces the previous one. The place of a photo is described separately, just below.
- **Who can see it**: only the members of the **same Miff** whose sharing is on, and **only if they also share theirs** (reciprocity). The creator of the Miff has no extra rights. People who are not in the Miff see nothing.
- **When it is erased**: when you stop sharing with that Miff, when you leave it, when the creator removes you, when the Miff is deleted, and when you delete your account. It is erased at the same time as your status and your photo, without depending on a delayed automatic process.
- **Display after 24 hours**: the app only shows a location older than 24 hours hidden, with its date always visible. Please note: "hidden" does not mean "erased". It stays stored on our servers until it is replaced or erased, in the cases above (see article 7).
- **Never anywhere else**: your location is never sent to Google Analytics or Crashlytics, nor written in the app's technical logs, nor in a sharing message.

**The place of your photo, in detail.**
- **What is saved**: the latitude, the longitude and the time of **its own measurement**, taken by your phone at the moment you take the photo (it is not necessarily your last known location, which may be older or more recent). A photo is **always published with its place**: there is no such thing as a photo without a location. This is a second copy of a position, attached to the image, which may differ from your last known location for 24 hours. There is still no history: one photo, so one photo place, per Miff you share with.
- **Who can see it**: only the members of the **same Miff** whose sharing is on, **and only if they also share their own location** (reciprocity). As a result, a member who does not share their location cannot open your photo. The creator of the Miff has no extra rights.
- **When it is erased**: with the photo. Unreadable after 24 hours, then deleted from our servers as soon as possible; also erased when you stop sharing with the Miff, leave it, are removed, or when the Miff or your account is deleted (see article 7: physical deletion at exactly the 24th hour is not guaranteed).
- **What we cannot verify**: our servers cannot check that the location or the time sent by a phone are true, or that the photo matches the place shown.
- **A copy is possible**: a member who sees your photo can take a screenshot of it, or save it on their phone with the "Save this photo" icon (only if they choose to; the saved file does not contain the place of the photo). We cannot erase any of these copies, and the 24 hours do not apply to them. Only publish what you are comfortable showing your circle.
- **Never anywhere else**: like your last known location, the place of your photo is never sent to Google Analytics or Crashlytics, nor written in the app's technical logs.

**Your consent.**
- Before any system permission request, Miff explains in the app what location is used for, including the fact that it works even when the app is closed.
- **This agreement is separate from your click on "Join"**: accepting the terms and statistics never shares your location.
- Sharing with a Miff only turns on if you decide so. The agreement is given **Miff by Miff**, and it is recorded with its date and the version of the text you saw. Nobody can turn it on for you.
- **Your location is only saved after you agree to a consent text of version 3 or a more recent one**, which states clearly that the members of your Miff see your last known location **and the place of your photos**. If you had accepted an older version of the text (one that did not mention location, or the place of the photo), Miff asks for your agreement again before saving your location or publishing a photo.
- You can **withdraw your consent at any time**, Miff by Miff, in the app settings (stop sharing), or by removing the location permission in your phone settings. As soon as you stop sharing with a Miff, your status, your photo (with its place) and your last location for that Miff are deleted.
- **Please note**: because Miff does not work without "Always" location, there is no way to use the app without sharing your location, and a photo is always published with its place. If you do not wish to share your location, you can choose not to use Miff, or delete your account.

**No hidden tracking.** Miff is not made to follow someone without their knowledge. The person concerned must know and agree. And your account works on **one phone at a time only**, so that an old phone does not send your circle a status that no longer matches you.

## 5. Who sees what?

The members of a Miff see:
- your first name, last name, profile photo (if you signed up with Google, the Google photo taken over, which you can change or remove before saving your profile and then at any time, even if you had never chosen a photo in Miff) and **phone number** (visible to the members of each of your Miffs, whether or not you share your status);
- the Miff's trusted places: name, **address**, position on the map, radius, type and category;
- **if you share**: your status (in a place, outside a place, in a danger zone, traveling…), the time, **your last known location with the time it was measured** (only if they also share their location), your photo if you published one in the last 24 hours, with **the place where it was taken and the time of that measurement** (only if they also share their location: a member who does not share their location cannot open a photo), your battery level and the state of your location permission;
- if you do not share, that your sharing is turned off.

To see other people's status, location and photos, you must share yours. People who are not in the Miff see nothing. **A member can take a screenshot of what they see** (a photo and its place, for example), **or save a photo on their phone with the "Save this photo" icon, only if they choose to**: we cannot prevent it, and "unreadable after 24 hours" applies to the app, not to a screenshot or a saved photo. The creator of the Miff has **no extra right to read** your status or your location. Anyone who knows a Miff's secret code can join it: only give it to people you trust, and be aware that its members see the address of the Miff's places.

The Miff team's access to the database is limited to authorised people and to what is necessary (maintenance, support, security).

**Your loved ones are told when you give news.** When you publish a photo, the other members of your Miff who share their location and kept notifications turned on receive a notification that contains your first name (see article 3). It contains neither the photo, nor its place, nor a place name, nor your Miff's name. People who are not in the Miff receive nothing.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 7. How long do we keep your data?

Miff keeps no history: the status, the last known location and the photo (with its place) are "current states", overwritten at each event.

## 8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to:
- **access** your data;
- have inaccurate data **rectified** (you can edit your profile in the app);
- have your data **erased**;
- **receive** your data in a reusable format (portability);
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest;
- ask for **restriction** of processing;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time (location, notifications, statistics, crash reports), without affecting what was done before;
- set **directives** on what happens to your data after your death (French law).

**How to do it:**
- **Delete your account**: directly in the app, in the settings. This deletes your account and the associated data described above. If you are the **creator** of a Miff, deleting your account also deletes that Miff, with its places, statuses, last known locations and photos. The other members then lose access to it: the app simply tells them, without any alarm, that the Miff no longer exists, and the data about them in that Miff, which has become unreadable, is erased the next time they open their app. Their own account is not affected and they can create or join another Miff. If you wish, let your loved ones know before deleting your account.
- **Stop sharing your location**: in the app settings, Miff by Miff. This deletes your status, your photo with its place and your last known location for that Miff at the same time.
- **Notifications**: in the app's Settings > Notifications ("When a loved one gives news" switch, general switch) or in your phone's settings. Turning off the general switch, signing out or deleting your account deletes your token (article 7).
- **Statistics and crash reports**: a single switch in the app Settings (the two are linked). Turning it off stops sending from that moment; what has already been sent is kept according to the periods in article 7.
- **Access or export your data**: during the test phase (beta), write to us at **tomessa.mfouka@gmail.com**. We reply with your data in a readable format.
- **Report a photo or other content**: write to us at **tomessa.mfouka@gmail.com**, stating the Miff and the first name of the member concerned. We check this address at least once a week; Miff is not an emergency service (see the Terms of Use).
- **Any other request**: write to us at **tomessa.mfouka@gmail.com**. We may ask you to confirm your identity, to prevent someone from impersonating you.

We reply within **one month** at most (extendable in the cases provided by law).

**Complaint.** If you think your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the **CNIL** (the French data protection authority), at www.cnil.fr, or with the authority of your country of residence. We would prefer that you write to us first, but you are not required to.

## 9. Security

We protect your data with appropriate measures: strict access rules (only members of a Miff can access that Miff's information; a member's last location and the place of their photo can only be read by the members of the same Miff whose sharing is on and who also share theirs), encrypted connections, encryption at rest provided by Google, verification that requests come from the genuine app (App Check), and access to administration tools limited to authorised people. ## 10. Minors

Miff does not impose a minimum age and does not ask for your age. Miff is designed for circles of loved ones (family, friends, partners), which may include young people. In France, the law provides that **below the age of 15**, the agreement of a parent or holder of parental authority is required for certain online services relying on consent. Miff cannot verify its users' age.

If you are a minor, we advise you to use Miff with your parents' agreement. If you are a parent and think that a child's data was collected without your agreement, write to **tomessa.mfouka@gmail.com**: we will delete their account and data.

Each member gives their own consent to share their location, and can stop at any time. This also applies to a minor: if they use Miff and share, their last known location, their photos and the place where they were taken are visible to the other members of their Miff, like those of any member, and those members receive a notification with their first name when they publish a photo. A member can also keep a copy (a screenshot, or saving the photo on their phone, only if they choose to): that copy escapes the 24 hours and we cannot erase it.

**Please note:** Miff does not verify its users' age. We therefore cannot check whether a parent's agreement was given where the law requires it.
