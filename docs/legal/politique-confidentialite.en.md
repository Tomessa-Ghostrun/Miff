# Miff Privacy Policy

**Version 0.1 — last updated: November 4, 2026**

Miff is an app that lets you know your loved ones are okay, without bothering them. To make that work, Miff processes some personal data, including information related to your location. This page explains, simply, what is collected, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you stay in control.

## 1. The essentials in 10 points

1. **Miff keeps your last known location, and only that.** This means the latitude, the longitude and the time of the measurement, saved on our servers (Firebase, Paris region). There is no history of your trips: each new location replaces the previous one.
2. **This is not live tracking.** Your phone itself watches the trusted places. Your location is saved at certain moments (for example when you open or leave the app, after a significant move, or when you arrive at a trusted place), and your loved ones see the last saved location, with the time.
3. **Sharing is your choice, Miff by Miff.** Nothing is saved without your explicit consent, and you can stop at any time.
4. **Only the members of the same Miff who also share can see your location**: to see other people's, you must share yours. The creator of the Miff has no more rights than the other members.
5. **Your location is erased** when you stop sharing, leave the Miff, are removed from the Miff, or when the Miff or your account is deleted. **After 24 hours**, the app only shows it hidden, with its date; it nevertheless stays stored until it is replaced or erased (see article 7).
6. **Miff needs "Always" location** (in the background, even when the app is closed) to work: this is what lets it notice your arrivals and departures without you having to open the app. Notifications, on the other hand, are optional.
7. **Update photos disappear after 24 hours.**
8. **Usage statistics and crash reports are turned on when you click "Join"** (or "Continue with Google") at sign-up (there is no checkbox), and you can turn them off at any time in Settings, as a single choice. Before that click, nothing is sent. **Your location is never sent to Google Analytics or Crashlytics.**
9. **No advertising, no selling of data.**
10. **One account, one phone at a time**, and you can delete your account from the app and write to us to exercise your rights.

## 4. Location and your position, explained

This is the most sensitive part, so here is everything.

**Why Miff asks for "Always" location (in the background, even when the app is closed).** To automatically tell your circle that you have arrived at a trusted place or left it, without you having to open the app. **Miff needs this permission ("Always", with precise location) to work**: until it is granted, the app asks for it again and the permission screen stays displayed. You can withdraw it at any time in your phone settings; the app will then ask for it again. Withdrawing this permission has no consequence on your account, but Miff cannot work without it. Notifications are optional.

**How it works.**
- Miff uses the "region monitoring" features of iOS and Android: your phone itself detects when you enter or leave a trusted place. To choose which places to watch (the 20 closest to you, because of a limit on phones), it reads its position at certain moments and keeps a copy on the phone.
- Miff **does not track your position continuously** and **does not build a history of your trips**.
- When your sharing is on, your phone writes your status (for example "at home") and your **last known location** in each Miff you share with. The app writes there at certain moments: when opened, when closed, after a significant move, when arriving at a trusted place. Your loved ones therefore see the last **saved** location, with the time it was measured: it may be several hours old and is not your live position.

**Your last known location, in detail.**
- **What is saved**: the latitude, the longitude and the time of the measurement, nothing else. No history: each location replaces the previous one.
- **Who can see it**: only the members of the **same Miff** whose sharing is on, and **only if they also share theirs** (reciprocity). The creator of the Miff has no extra rights. People who are not in the Miff see nothing.
- **When it is erased**: when you stop sharing with that Miff, when you leave it, when the creator removes you, when the Miff is deleted, and when you delete your account. It is erased at the same time as your status and your update photo, without depending on a delayed automatic process.
- **Display after 24 hours**: the app only shows a location older than 24 hours hidden, with its date always visible. Please note: "hidden" does not mean "erased". It stays stored on our servers until it is replaced or erased, in the cases above (see article 7).
- **Never anywhere else**: your location is never sent to Google Analytics or Crashlytics, nor written in the app's technical logs, nor in a sharing message.

**Your consent.**
- Before any system permission request, Miff explains in the app what location is used for, including the fact that it works even when the app is closed.
- **This agreement is separate from your click on "Join"**: accepting the terms and statistics never shares your location.
- Sharing with a Miff only turns on if you decide so. The agreement is given **Miff by Miff**, and it is recorded with its date and the version of the text you saw. Nobody can turn it on for you.
- **Your location is only saved after you agree to a consent text of version 3 or a more recent one**, which states clearly that the members of your Miff see your last known location. If you had accepted an older version of the text (one that did not mention location), Miff asks for your agreement again before saving your location.
- You can **withdraw your consent at any time**, Miff by Miff, in the app settings (stop sharing), or by removing the location permission in your phone settings. As soon as you stop sharing with a Miff, your status, your update photo and your last location for that Miff are deleted.
- **Please note**: because Miff does not work without "Always" location, there is no way to use the app with manual updates only. If you do not wish to share your location, you can choose not to use Miff, or delete your account.

**No hidden tracking.** Miff is not made to follow someone without their knowledge. The person concerned must know and agree. And your account works on **one phone at a time only**, so that an old phone does not send your circle a status that no longer matches you.

## 5. Who sees what?

The members of a Miff see:
- your first name, last name, profile photo and **phone number** (visible to the members of each of your Miffs, whether or not you share your status);
- the Miff's trusted places: name, **address**, position on the map, radius, type and category;
- **if you share**: your status (in a place, outside a place, in a risk zone…), the time, **your last known location with the time it was measured** (only if they also share their location), your optional update (text, photo), your battery level and the state of your location permission;
- if you do not share, that your sharing is turned off.

To see other people's status and location, you must share yours. People who are not in the Miff see nothing. The creator of the Miff has **no extra right to read** your status or your location. Anyone who knows a Miff's secret code can join it: only give it to people you trust, and be aware that its members see the address of the Miff's places.

The Miff team's access to the database is limited to authorised people and to what is necessary (maintenance, support, security).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to:
- **access** your data;
- have inaccurate data **rectified** (you can edit your profile in the app);
- have your data **erased**;
- **receive** your data in a reusable format (portability);
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest;
- ask for **restriction** of processing;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time (location, statistics, crash reports), without affecting what was done before;
- set **directives** on what happens to your data after your death (French law).

**How to do it:**
- **Delete your account**: directly in the app, in the settings. This deletes your account and the associated data described above. If you are the **creator** of a Miff, deleting your account also deletes that Miff, with its places, statuses, last known locations and photos. The other members then lose access to it: the app simply tells them, without any alarm, that the Miff no longer exists, and the data about them in that Miff, which has become unreadable, is erased the next time they open their app. Their own account is not affected and they can create or join another Miff. If you wish, let your loved ones know before deleting your account.
- **Stop sharing your location**: in the app settings, Miff by Miff. This deletes your status, your update photo and your last known location for that Miff at the same time.
- **Statistics and crash reports**: a single switch in the app Settings (the two are linked). Turning it off stops sending from that moment; what has already been sent is kept according to the periods in article 7.
- **Access or export your data**: during the test phase (beta), write to us at **tomessa.mfouka@gmail.com**. We reply with your data in a readable format.
- **Any other request**: write to us at **tomessa.mfouka@gmail.com**. We may ask you to confirm your identity, to prevent someone from impersonating you.

We reply within **one month** at most (extendable in the cases provided by law).

**Complaint.** If you think your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the **CNIL** (the French data protection authority), at www.cnil.fr, or with the authority of your country of residence. We would prefer that you write to us first, but you are not required to.

## 9. Security

We protect your data with appropriate measures: strict access rules (only members of a Miff can access that Miff's information; a member's last location can only be read by the members of the same Miff whose sharing is on and who also share theirs), encrypted connections, encryption at rest provided by Google, verification that requests come from the genuine app (App Check), and access to administration tools limited to authorised people. No system is infallible. In the event of a data breach likely to harm you, we will notify the CNIL and, if necessary, you, as the law requires.

Also protect your phone (lock code) and only share a Miff's secret code with people you trust.

## 10. Minors

Miff does not impose a minimum age and does not ask for your age. Miff is designed for circles of loved ones (family, friends, partners), which may include young people. In France, the law provides that **below the age of 15**, the agreement of a parent or holder of parental authority is required for certain online services relying on consent. Miff cannot verify its users' age.

If you are a minor, we advise you to use Miff with your parents' agreement. If you are a parent and think that a child's data was collected without your agreement, write to **tomessa.mfouka@gmail.com**: we will delete their account and data.

Each member gives their own consent to share their location, and can stop at any time. This also applies to a minor: if they use Miff and share, their last known location is visible to the other members of their Miff, like that of any member.

**Please note:** Miff does not verify its users' age. We therefore cannot check whether a parent's agreement was given where the law requires it.
